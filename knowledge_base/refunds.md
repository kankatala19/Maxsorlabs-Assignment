# Refunds

Refunds are available for 30 days from the delivery date when the customer provides a valid order number.

Approved refunds are returned only to the original payment method. Store credit is not a default option.

Full refunds are allowed when:
- The item is unused and in original packaging, or
- The item never arrived after an investigation, or
- A damaged item is verified with photos and a replacement is not wanted.

Partial refunds may be offered for missing accessories when the main item is kept.

Do not refund:
- Items past 30 days from delivery
- Digital products after they have been downloaded
- Gift cards
- Items marked final sale

If the order number or delivery date is missing, request more information before deciding.
If policy does not clearly apply, return NEEDS_MORE_INFORMATION.
