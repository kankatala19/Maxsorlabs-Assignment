# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days after the order ships.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

A delay investigation can start only when the customer provides the order number and delivery address.

If tracking shows "in transit" and it is still within the delivery window, do not refund yet. Ask the customer to wait until the estimated delivery date plus 2 business days.

If tracking shows "delivered" but the customer did not receive it:
- Confirm the address
- Ask them to check with neighbors and the carrier
- If still missing after 3 days, open a lost-package claim
- Replacement or refund may be approved after the claim is opened

Do not change the shipping address after the label has been created.
International orders cannot be redirected once they leave the origin warehouse.

If the package is delayed more than 10 business days with no tracking updates, offer a replacement or refund.
